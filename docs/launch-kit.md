## Walkthrough shot list (40–50 seconds)

Record the actual website at 1920 × 1080 with notifications hidden and the cursor visible. Load the journey once before the final take. Keep one continuous recording as evidence; trim pauses for the social edit without speeding up UI responses misleadingly.

- 0–4s: Exterior and headline. Caption: “What if booking started before you picked a seat?”
- 4–17s: Scroll through the entrance, lobby and stage. Leave a beat at the stage reveal.
- 17–24s: Turn toward seating and briefly reverse the scroll.
- 24–35s: Open booking; select two available seats. Show quantity and total. Remove one, then add it again.
- 35–43s: Cut to the demo checkout and generated ticket using fictional attendee information. Keep the demo label visible.
- 43–50s: End card: “Stillwater — designed by Cold Designer” and the live URL. Caption: “Have a venue or experience worth bringing to life? Let’s talk.”

Before recording: verify every selection carries through, mobile scroll is acceptable on a physical phone, and the deployment contains the intended fixes. Do not claim the demonstration is a live ticketing service.

## LinkedIn draft

I didn’t want this to be another concert website where you see a poster and click “Book now.”

I wanted you to feel like you had already arrived.

That became Stillwater: a concept where scrolling takes you from outside the venue, through the lobby, to the stage and seating. From there, you can choose seats and continue through a demo booking flow to a digital ticket.

The tricky part was making the atmosphere and the practical decisions work together. The entrance should feel inviting, but the price still needs to be clear. The camera can move, but choosing a seat needs to feel immediate.

I led the concept, visual direction and interaction decisions, using AI-generated footage and Codex to help bring the experience into the browser.

It is a concept project, with sample availability and simulated checkout. I’m continuing to refine the mobile experience.

## Portfolio project entry

Title: Stillwater — an immersive concert booking concept

Summary: A cinematic venue journey paired with clear seat selection and a complete demo booking flow.

Role: Product design, art direction, interaction design, AI-assisted implementation.

Audience: Concert venues and event organizers exploring more expressive ways to introduce a space and sell tickets.

Design question: How might visitors experience the atmosphere of a venue while still making clear, practical booking decisions?

Approach: A six-clip scroll-controlled journey introduces the exterior, lobby, stage and seating. HTML overlays provide context. A separate schematic seat map keeps availability and selection stable while the footage supplies atmosphere. The booking flow supports multiple seats and a sample ticket.

Tradeoffs: The footage is prerendered, rather than a freely navigable 3D venue. The map uses sample inventory and is not an exact venue plan. Checkout is simulated. Mobile performance requires testing on physical devices.

Result: A working interactive concept. No conversion uplift, sales, or user-research outcome is claimed.

Project link: https://stillwater-concert-experience.vercel.app/

Contact CTA: “Have a venue or experience worth bringing to life? Let’s talk.”
