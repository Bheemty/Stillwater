When should I arrive?+

For this sample evening, doors open at 18:30 and the programme begins at 19:30. Allow time to explore the lobby and find your seat. All dates and times are illustrative.

Can I choose seats on either side?+

Yes. The seat map includes left, centre and right blocks across front, middle and rear zones. Every seat has its own ID and sample price. This schematic demonstration is not a verified floor plan of the filmed hall.

Is this a real booking?+

No. Select seats, enter sample attendee details, simulate payment and create a digital ticket. Nothing is charged or reserved. Demo tickets are not valid for entry.

Can I explore without motion?+

Choose “Still views” for static scenes with chapter navigation. The complete booking flow remains available. Your device’s reduced-motion preference is respected automatically.
